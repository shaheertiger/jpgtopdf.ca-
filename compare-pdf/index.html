Compare PDF — Find Every Text Difference Between Two Versions

Add the original PDF, then the changed one, and press Compare to see every edit.

Compare PDF

Quick answer

Add the original PDF first and the changed version second, then press Compare. Every word that was added shows in green and every word removed in red, with the page it is on. Changes that involve numbers, dates or words like "not" and "never" are listed first under Check these first. It compares text only, not layout, images or formatting.

How to compare two PDF files

  1. Add the original PDF. It becomes A, the old version.
  2. Add the changed PDF. It becomes B, the new version. Got them backwards? Press Swap; this is the step people get wrong, and it flips green and red.
  3. Press Compare. The summary shows how many changes there are and how many words were added and removed.
  4. Read Check these first. Changed amounts, dates and negations are listed with their page, so the edits that matter most aren't lost in reworded sentences.
  5. Download the report if you need to share it. Download report (HTML) saves the results as a page you can open in any browser or print to PDF.

How do I find what changed between two versions of a contract?

Add the version you agreed to as A and the version you were sent as B. The tool lines up the text, then marks each inserted and deleted word. Read the flagged list first: a payment that went from 30 days to 15, a date moved by a month, or a "not" added to a clause can change the meaning of a whole page.

Which changes does Compare PDF flag?

Every other change is still shown in the full list; the flags just bring these to the top.

Can I compare scanned PDFs?

Not directly. A scan is pictures of pages, so there are no words to compare, and the tool tells you when a file has no text. Run each scan through OCR PDF first, then compare the two searchable PDFs. OCR can misread a letter now and then, so a few false differences may appear.

Use cases

Contract redlines from the other side

The other party sends a "clean" revised contract with no tracked changes. Compare it with your last version to see every edit they made, including quiet ones.

Lease renewal

Check a renewal lease against last year's: rent amount, notice period, pet and parking clauses. Number flags catch the rent change at once.

Insurance policy and terms updates

Compare this year's policy wording with last year's to spot new exclusions. Negation flags catch "except" and "not covered" additions.

Job offer revisions

Confirm that only the salary line changed between offer letters, and nothing in the non-compete or start date.

Frequently asked questions

Is Compare PDF free?

Yes. It is free, with no account and no limit on how many comparisons you run.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. Both PDFs are read and compared in your browser. That matters for contracts and offer letters you shouldn't send to a third party.

Does it compare images or layout?

No. It compares the words only. A moved picture or a new font does not show up as a change.

Can it compare a Word file with a PDF?

Save the Word file as PDF first, then compare the two PDFs.

What does the HTML report contain?

The summary, the flagged changes and every change block with its page reference, in one file you can open in a browser or print to PDF.

Can I compare more than two PDFs?

No. It compares two at a time. Adding a third file replaces version B.
