Split PDF by Size — Keep Every Part Under Your Limit

Drop a PDF, set the maximum size per file, and download the parts in a ZIP.

Split PDF by size

Quick answer

Type a maximum size, such as 4 MB, and the tool fills each part with whole pages, in order, until the next page would push it over the limit. It measures every part as it builds it, so the sizes it reports are the real ones. Pages are never cut. A single page that is bigger than the limit on its own goes into its own part and is flagged.

How to split a PDF by size

  1. Drop your PDF. The tool shows its size and page count.
  2. Set the maximum size per file. Type a number in MB or pick a preset. Use the portal's exact limit; the tool keeps each part at or under it.
  3. Press Split by size. It checks page by page and reports progress as it goes.
  4. Read the result line before uploading. It lists each part with its page range and size. If a page is flagged as too big on its own, that part will still be over the limit: compress the PDF first, then split.

How do I split a PDF into 4 MB parts?

Set the limit to 4, or choose 4 MB (IRCC) from the presets, and press Split by size. You get a ZIP with parts that are each 4 MB or less, named in order so you can upload them as part 1, part 2 and so on.

Why are the parts bigger in total than the original PDF?

Fonts and images shared by many pages are stored once in the original. Each part needs its own copy of the fonts it uses, so the parts together can add up to a little more than the original file. Each part still stays under your limit.

Use cases

IRCC (4 MB per file)

Immigration uploads often cap each document at 4 MB. Split a long bank statement or a set of scanned pages into numbered parts that each pass.

Outlook (20 MB) and Gmail (25 MB)

Send a large report across several emails, one part per message, instead of using a file-sharing link the recipient can't open.

Court e-filing

Many e-filing systems limit each attachment. Splitting by size keeps exhibits in page order across parts.

Job and school application portals

Portals with a 2 MB or 5 MB cap reject a full transcript or portfolio. Split it to the cap and upload each part.

Frequently asked questions

Will splitting by size cut a page in half?

No. Each part contains whole pages only, in their original order.

Is it free and private?

Yes. It is free with no account, and the PDF is split in your browser without being uploaded.

What if a single page is bigger than my limit?

That page goes into a part on its own, and the result line names it. Compress the PDF first to bring that page under the limit.

Do the parts lose quality?

No. Pages are copied as they are, without re-rendering or recompressing.

Why do I always get a ZIP?

The parts come in one ZIP so they download together and stay in order. Unzip it to get the separate PDFs.

Is there a maximum PDF size?

There is no set cap; your device's memory is the limit. Long files take longer because each part is measured as it is built, and a laptop handles big files better than a phone.
