Rotate PDF — Turn Pages and Save Them Permanently

Add a PDF, turn the pages that are sideways or upside down, and save.

Rotate PDF

Quick answer

To rotate a PDF, add the file, tap ↻ on each page that needs turning (90° clockwise per tap), or use All left, All right or 180° All for the whole document. Press Save rotated PDF. Only each page's rotation setting changes, so nothing is redrawn or blurred, and the rotation stays when the file is opened anywhere. Nothing is uploaded.

How to rotate a PDF

  1. Add the PDF. Tap Select a PDF or drop it on the box. A thumbnail appears for every page.
  2. Rotate the pages that need it. Tap ↻ on a page to turn it 90° clockwise; the thumbnail turns so you can check it.
  3. Or rotate everything. Use ↺ All left, ↻ All right or 180° All. Reset puts every page back.
  4. Check before saving. Look at the thumbnails once more: a page turned one tap too many is the common mistake.
  5. Press Save rotated PDF and download.

How do I rotate a PDF and save it permanently?

Rotating in a PDF viewer usually only changes your view, and the file opens sideways again next time. This tool writes the new angle into the file, so every reader, email client and upload portal shows the page upright.

How do I rotate only one page?

Tap ↻ on that page only and save. The other pages are left exactly as they were. For rotating and reordering at the same time, use Organize PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Does rotating a PDF reduce quality?

No. Only the page's rotation setting changes; the content is not redrawn, so it stays exactly as sharp.

Is the PDF uploaded?

No. Your browser rotates the pages on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Will other apps show the new rotation?

Yes. The angle is saved in the file, so Adobe Reader, Preview, browsers and upload portals all show it.

Are links and form fields kept?

Yes. The whole file is kept and only the rotation of the pages you changed is updated.

Can I rotate pages on my phone?

Yes, in Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android.

Can I rotate by 45 degrees?

No. PDF pages turn in steps of 90°, which is what readers and printers support.
