You took a photo on your Android phone and now a form, a landlord or an employer wants it as a PDF.

**Every Android phone can do it without an app:** open the photo in Google Photos, tap **⋮** → **Print**, set the printer to **Save as PDF** and tap the PDF button. For several photos in one PDF, open [our free converter](/) in Chrome, select them and tap Download PDF.

The catch is that "Save as PDF" is hidden as a fake printer, so nobody looks for it there. This guide covers three free methods, how they differ on Samsung and Pixel phones, and what to do when the PDF comes out too big.

## Why Is "Save as PDF" Hidden in the Print Menu?

Android treats PDF creation as printing to a file. The system print service includes a virtual printer called **Save as PDF**, available in any app with a Print option: Google Photos, Chrome, Gmail, Docs.

### What Most People Don't Know

Samsung's Gallery app and Google Photos both reach the same Android print service, but the menu path differs. Samsung puts Print under the three-dot menu or in Share. Menu labels also change between Android and One UI versions, so look for the nearest match.

> **Key point:** If an app can print, it can make a PDF. Look for Print, then switch the printer to Save as PDF.

## Method 1: Google Photos → Print → Save as PDF

### Step 1: Open the Photo

Open **Google Photos** and tap the photo. To include several, long-press the first, tap the others, then use the Share or ⋮ menu.

### Step 2: Tap ⋮, Then Print

Tap **⋮** (More) at the top right and choose **Print**. On some versions it's under **Share** → **Print**.

### Step 3: Choose "Save as PDF", Not a Printer

At the top of the print screen, tap the printer name and choose **Save as PDF**. This is the step people miss: if a real printer is selected, you'll print paper instead.

> **Pro tip:** Set **Paper size** to Letter before saving if the document will be printed in Canada or the US. Letter is 8.5 × 11 inches.

### Step 4: Tap the PDF Button and Save

Tap the round **PDF** button, pick Downloads or Drive, rename the file and tap **Save**.

## Method 2: Chrome and a Browser Converter

1. Open [jpgtopdf.ca](/) in Chrome.
2. Tap **Select images** and pick photos from Gallery or Files.
3. Choose page size (Fit to image, Letter, A4) and margin.
4. Tap **Download PDF**. It lands in Downloads.

Conversion happens in Chrome on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, which matters for IDs and bank documents.

## Method 3: Google Drive Scan (For Paper)

For paper documents, scan instead of photographing:

1. Open **Google Drive** and tap **+** (or the camera icon).
2. Choose **Scan** and photograph the page. Drive crops and flattens it.
3. Add more pages with **+**, then **Save**. The result is a PDF in Drive.

See [how to scan a document to PDF with your phone](/blog/scan-document-to-pdf-phone/) for tips on lighting and size.

## PDF Converter Apps From the Play Store (Use With Caution)

Search "JPG to PDF" in the Play Store and you'll find dozens of apps. Some are fine; many are ad-heavy, ask for access to all your files, or upload images to a server.

### The Real Risks

- Broad storage permissions for a job that needs one photo.
- Full-screen ads between steps, sometimes with misleading buttons.
- Watermarks or page limits until you pay.

Checklist before installing: recent updates, a clear privacy policy, no "all files" permission, works offline.

> **Bottom line:** Android already has Save as PDF built in. Install an app only if you convert dozens of files a day.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I save a picture as a PDF on Android?

Open it in Google Photos, tap ⋮ → Print, choose Save as PDF as the printer and tap the PDF button. It's built into Android.

### Can I convert JPG to PDF on Samsung without an app?

Yes. In Gallery or Google Photos, open the photo, choose Print and pick Save as PDF. Or use a browser converter in Chrome or Samsung Internet.

### How do I combine multiple JPGs into one PDF on Android?

Select them all in a browser converter; each becomes a page. Google Photos can print several photos too, but the order is harder to control.

### Where do PDFs save on Android?

Usually in Downloads. Open the Files app (Files by Google or My Files on Samsung) and look in Downloads.

### Is Save as PDF free?

Yes. It's part of Android's print service, with no watermark.

### Does Google Drive convert JPG to PDF?

Drive's Scan button creates a PDF from the camera. It doesn't convert a JPG already in your gallery; use Print or a browser converter for that.
