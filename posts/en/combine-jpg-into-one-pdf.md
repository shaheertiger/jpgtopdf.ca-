## Quick answer

To combine images into one PDF, select all of them in the converter below (JPG, PNG and iPhone HEIC can be mixed), drag the thumbnails into page order, and press **Download PDF**. Each image becomes one page of a single file. On **Original**, the PDF weighs the total of your photos plus under 1 KB per page: five 3 MB photos make a 15 MB PDF.

The portal has one upload button and you have six photos: the front and back of an ID, three pages of a lease, a void cheque. The converter above puts them in one PDF in about 30 seconds, and nothing is uploaded.

Built-in tools can combine pictures too, but they choose the page order for you, usually by file name. That's how a lease ends up with page 3 first. This guide shows how to keep the order right, keep the file small enough to upload, and do it on every device.

## How to Combine Multiple JPGs Into One PDF

1. **Select every image at once.** Tap **Select images** above and pick them all: Ctrl- or Cmd-click on a computer, tap each one on a phone. Selecting again adds more to the end.
2. **Drag them into order; don't trust the file names.** The thumbnails are numbered. Drag them, or use the ← and → buttons. For two-sided cards, put the front first, then the back.
3. **Make the pages consistent.** Choose **Letter** (Canada, US) or **A4** so every page is the same size even if the photos aren't. **Fit to image** keeps each page the size of its photo, which looks uneven when portrait and landscape shots are mixed.
4. **Pick the quality.** **Original** for the sharpest file; **Balanced** when the PDF has to go by email or through an upload form.
5. **Download and check.** Press **Download PDF**, scroll through every page, and check the size before you upload.

## How Big Will the Combined PDF Be?

On Original, the JPEGs go into the PDF byte for byte, so the size is simple arithmetic. We measured the PDF's own overhead with the same library the converter uses: 0.9 KB for one page, 4 KB in total for ten pages. The table adds it to the photos.

| Photos | 2 MB each | 3 MB each | 5 MB each |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 4.0 MB | 6.0 MB | 10.0 MB |
| 5 | 10.0 MB | 15.0 MB | 25.0 MB |
| 10 | 20.0 MB | 30.0 MB | 50.0 MB |
| 20 | 40.0 MB | 60.0 MB | 100.0 MB |

So at 3 MB a photo, Outlook's 20 MB attachment cap fits 6 photos and Gmail's 25 MB fits 8. Over that, choose **Balanced** before downloading: it caps the long edge at 2,480 pixels, which is still about 225 dpi on a full A4 or Letter page, and is typically 60–80% smaller for phone photos. If the PDF is already made, see [how to shrink a JPG to PDF that's too big](/blog/jpg-to-pdf-file-size/).

> **Key point:** Order and size are the two things that go wrong. Fix the order in the converter, and check the size before the upload fails, not after.

## How Do I Combine Photos Into One PDF on iPhone, Android, Windows or Mac?

Every system has a built-in way. The catch is in the last column.

| Device | Built-in way | Page order follows |
|---|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 | Select files → right-click → Print → Microsoft Print to PDF | Folder sort order |
| Mac | Select files → Quick Actions → Create PDF | File name |
| iPhone/iPad | Files → Select → ⋯ → Create PDF | File name |
| Android | Google Photos → select → Print → Save as PDF | Selection, varies |

Device guides: [Windows](/blog/jpg-to-pdf-windows/), [Mac](/blog/jpg-to-pdf-mac/), [iPhone](/blog/jpg-to-pdf-iphone/), [Android](/blog/jpg-to-pdf-android/). To use a built-in tool and still control the order, rename the files 01, 02, 03… first.

## How Do I Save a Folder of JPG Images Into One File?

Open the folder, select everything (Ctrl+A on Windows, Cmd+A on a Mac), and drag the whole selection onto the converter box above. The images are added in the order your system hands them over, usually by name, and you can reorder before downloading. The result is one PDF with one image per page, which is the usual answer when a form or an email wants "one file".

## Combining Problems and Fixes

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Pages out of order | Drag thumbnails, or rename files 01, 02… | Built-in tools sort by name |
| Mixed page sizes | Pick Letter or A4 instead of Fit to image | Every page gets the same size |
| Too big to upload | Balanced or Small preset, or fewer photos | On Original, size = sum of photos |
| Sideways page | Tap ↻ on that thumbnail | The photo was stored sideways with no rotation tag |
| Need to add a page later | Combine again with the new image | Rebuilding is quicker than editing |

## Merging PDFs vs Combining Images

If some pages are already PDFs (a bank statement, a signed form), combine your images into one PDF first, then join the PDFs with [Merge PDF on pdfcompact.com](https://pdfcompact.com/merge-pdf), our sister site. The converter here accepts images only.

## Upload-Style Merge Sites (Use With Caution)

People combine images to send identity or financial documents, exactly what you don't want sitting on a stranger's server. Upload sites keep a server copy, often cap free use at 20 pages or 2 files, and some stamp a watermark on every page. Look for browser-only processing, no sign-up and no watermark.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I combine multiple JPGs into one PDF for free?

Select all the JPGs in the converter at the top of this page, drag them into order and download one PDF. It's free, with no sign-up or watermark, and the files never leave your device.

### How do I combine photos into one PDF on iPhone?

Open this page in Safari, tap Select images, pick the photos from your library (HEIC is fine) and download. Or use the Files app: select the images → ⋯ → Create PDF, which orders pages by file name.

### How do I combine pictures into one PDF on Windows?

Select the pictures in File Explorer, right-click → Print → Microsoft Print to PDF, and untick Fit picture to frame so nothing is cropped. Or drag them onto the converter above to choose the order.

### Can I put JPG and PNG in the same PDF?

Yes. Each image becomes its own page whatever its format. JPEGs are copied unchanged and PNGs are stored losslessly.

### How do I merge a JPG and a PDF together?

Turn the JPG into a PDF here, then merge the two PDFs with a merge tool such as Merge PDF on pdfcompact.com. A PDF can't be dropped into this converter.

### How many images can I put in one PDF?

There is no set limit; your device's memory is. A laptop handles dozens of phone photos easily. Upload portals limit the file size, not the page count.

### Why is my combined PDF so big?

On Original its size is the total of all the images: ten 3 MB photos make a 30 MB PDF. Choose Balanced or Small before downloading to cut it down.
