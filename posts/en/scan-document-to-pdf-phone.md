You need to send a signed form, a lease or a doctor's note as a PDF, and there's no scanner in sight.

**Your phone already has one.** On iPhone, open **Files** → **⋯** → **Scan Documents** (or Notes → attachment icon → Scan Documents). On Android, open **Google Drive** → **+** → **Scan**. The phone finds the page edges, flattens it and saves a PDF. No app to install.

A plain photo of a page and a scan look similar, but a scan is cropped, straightened, evenly lit and much smaller. This guide covers both phones, the setup that gives the cleanest result, and what to do if you only have photos.

## Scan vs Photo: What's the Difference?

A photo captures everything in the frame: the table, shadows, a tilted angle. A scanner app detects the page, corrects the perspective so it looks flat, boosts contrast, and crops the rest away.

### What Most People Don't Know

Scanned pages are often **5–10 times smaller** than photos of the same page, because the background is gone and the image is cleaned up. That's often the difference between passing and failing an upload limit.

> **Key point:** For paper, scan. For things that aren't flat pages (an object, a screen, a whiteboard), take a photo and convert it.

## How to Scan to PDF on iPhone

### Step 1: Open the Scanner

- **Files app:** tap **⋯** at the top right → **Scan Documents**. The PDF saves straight into the folder you're in.
- **Notes app:** open a note → tap the attachment (paper clip) or camera icon → **Scan Documents**.

### Step 2: Let It Capture

Hold the phone above the page. In Auto mode it captures as soon as it finds the edges. Switch to Manual if it keeps capturing too early.

### Step 3: Add Pages, Then Save

Keep scanning for more pages. Tap a thumbnail to crop, rotate or change the filter (Colour, Greyscale, Black & White). Tap **Save**.

> **Pro tip:** Use **Greyscale** or **Black & White** for text-only documents. The file is smaller and easier to read. Keep **Colour** for IDs, stamps and anything with colour coding.

### Step 4: Rename and Share

In Files, long-press the PDF → Rename. In Notes, open the scan → Share → Save to Files.

## How to Scan to PDF on Android

1. Open **Google Drive** and tap **+** (or the camera icon on some versions).
2. Tap **Scan**. Point at the page and capture.
3. Adjust the crop, add pages with **+**, then tap **Save** or **Done**.
4. Name it and choose a folder. The PDF is in Drive; download it if you need it on the phone.

Samsung phones also offer document scanning in the Camera app (a "Scan" prompt appears when it detects a document). Menu labels change between versions.

## Get a Clean Scan First Time

| Setup | Do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Surface | Dark, plain, flat | The scanner finds white page edges easily |
| Light | Daylight from a window, no flash | Flash makes a glare spot |
| Angle | Phone parallel to the page | Less stretching to correct |
| Shadows | Keep your hand and phone out of the light path | Shadows look like smudges |
| Pages | Flatten folds under a book first | Curves bend the text |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Captures before you're ready | Switch to Manual | Auto fires as soon as it sees edges |
| Edges cut off | Drag the crop corners after capture | Detection isn't always exact |
| PDF too large | Use Greyscale, scan fewer pages per file | Colour pages are heavier, see [size guide](/blog/jpg-to-pdf-file-size/) |
| Signature too faint | Use Colour or Greyscale, not B&W | B&W can erase thin pen strokes |
| Text looks wavy | Flatten the page, rescan | Curved paper can't be fully corrected |

## Free Scanner Apps With Subscriptions (Use With Caution)

Third-party scanner apps often limit free scans, watermark pages, or require an account that syncs your documents to their cloud.

### The Real Risks

- Weekly subscriptions after a short trial.
- ID and tax documents stored in another company's cloud.

> **Bottom line:** iPhone and Android scanners are free and good enough for almost every form. Use them first.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I scan a document to PDF on my iPhone?

Open Files, tap ⋯ → Scan Documents, capture the pages and tap Save. The PDF is saved in the current folder.

### How do I scan to PDF on Android?

Open Google Drive, tap + → Scan, capture the pages and tap Save. Drive stores it as a PDF.

### Is a phone scan good enough for official documents?

Usually yes, if it's flat, sharp and fully in frame. Check the organization's requirements for colour or resolution.

### Should I scan in colour or black and white?

Colour for IDs, stamps and signatures; greyscale for text documents; black & white only for clean printed text.

### Can I scan several pages into one PDF?

Yes. Keep capturing before you tap Save; all pages go into one PDF.
