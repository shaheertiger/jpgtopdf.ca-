The upload form says "maximum 4 MB" and your PDF is 18 MB. Or Gmail refuses the attachment.

**Resize the photos before converting, not the PDF after.** A phone photo is usually 3,000–4,000 pixels wide and 2–5 MB. Resized to about 1,600 pixels on the long side, it drops to roughly 300–600 KB and stays perfectly readable. Convert those with [our free converter](/) and five pages fit comfortably under 4 MB.

People reach for "compress PDF" first. It works, but it re-saves every image at lower quality with no control over which pages get blurry. This guide shows the size maths, three ways to hit a limit, and the common limits you'll meet in Canada.

## Why Is a PDF From Photos So Big?

A PDF doesn't shrink images; it holds them. The file size is roughly the sum of the images inside, plus a little overhead.

### What Most People Don't Know

Phone cameras capture far more pixels than a document needs. A 12-megapixel photo of a receipt has the same readable information at 1,600 pixels wide as at 4,000, but costs about six times the storage.

> **Key point:** Resizing removes pixels you don't need; compressing degrades pixels you keep. Resize first.

Rough figures. Busy photos (patterned backgrounds, dark noisy shots) are bigger than clean white pages.

## Method 1: Resize the Photos, Then Convert

### Step 1: Resize Each Photo

- **iPhone:** use the Shortcuts app's "Resize Image" action, or take a smaller copy by screenshotting the photo full screen (about 1,200 px wide on most iPhones).
- **Android:** many Gallery apps, including Samsung Gallery, have a Resize option in the photo editor or the ⋮ menu.
- **Windows:** open in Photos → ⋯ → Resize image → choose a preset or custom 1,600 px.
- **Mac:** Preview → Tools → Adjust Size → width 1600 (keep proportions ticked).

Menu labels change between app versions.

### Step 2: Convert

Select the resized photos in [jpgtopdf.ca](/) and download. The converter keeps each JPG as-is, so the PDF size matches the photos you gave it.

> **Pro tip:** Photograph documents on a plain, flat, light surface. A busy tablecloth behind the page can double the file size.

## Method 2: Scan Instead of Photographing

Phone scanners (iPhone Files/Notes, Google Drive) crop to the page and clean up the background, which makes much smaller files. A scanned page is often 200–500 KB. See [scan a document to PDF with your phone](/blog/scan-document-to-pdf-phone/).

## Method 3: Compress the Finished PDF

If you already have the PDF and it's slightly over, use a PDF compressor such as [pdfcanada's Compress PDF](https://www.pdfcanada.ca/en/compress-pdf). Check the result at 200% zoom: compression can make small print hard to read.

## File Size Problems and Fixes

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| One page alone is 5 MB | Resize that photo to ~1600 px | It's a full-resolution photo |
| Still too big after resizing | Split into two PDFs if the form allows | Each file gets its own limit |
| Text unreadable after compressing | Go back, resize less, skip compression | Compression degraded small text |
| PNG pages are huge | Save photo-like PNGs as JPG first | PNG is lossless and heavy for photos |

## "Compress to Exactly X KB" Sites (Use With Caution)

Tools that promise an exact size often crush every image to get there.

### The Real Risks

- Unreadable numbers on IDs and statements, leading to rejected applications.
- Uploading sensitive documents to an unknown server.

> **Bottom line:** Resize on your device first. Compress only the last bit, and always check readability.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is my JPG to PDF file so big?

Phone photos are 2–5 MB each and the PDF is roughly the sum of its images.

### How do I make a PDF under 2 MB from photos?

Resize each photo to about 1,600 px on the long side, or scan the pages, then convert. Up to about five pages usually fits.

### Does resizing a photo make the text unreadable?

Not at 1,600 px for a normal document page. Check by zooming to 100%.

### Is it better to compress the PDF or resize the photos?

Resize the photos. You choose exactly how much detail to keep, and every page stays equally readable.

### Can I email a big PDF?

Gmail allows 25 MB per email. Larger files are sent as a Drive link instead.
