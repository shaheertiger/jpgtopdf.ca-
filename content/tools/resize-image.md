Drop your images, pick a preset such as **Instagram post — 1080×1080** or type a width and height, choose **Crop to fill** for an exact size, and press **Resize images**. Set only the width (or only the height) to keep the shape. PNG stays PNG, WebP stays WebP, everything else saves as JPG at quality 90.

## How to resize an image

1. **Add your images.** Several images get the same settings.
2. **Choose a preset or type a size.** A preset fills in width and height and sets Crop to fill. Leave one box empty to keep the original shape, or use the percentage box.
3. **Choose how to fit.** This is the decision that matters: **Crop to fill** gives the exact size and trims the edges from the centre; **Fit inside** keeps the whole picture but may come out smaller on one side; **white bars** keep the whole picture at the exact size.
4. **Resize and download.** Each file is named with its new size, for example `photo-1080x1080.jpg`.

## How do I resize without cropping?

Choose **Fit inside (no crop)**. The image is scaled until it fits within the width and height you set, so one side may be shorter than asked. If a platform needs the exact size, choose **Fit inside + white bars**: the whole picture is kept and the gap is filled with white.

## How do I resize by percentage?

Clear the width and height and type a number in **Or scale (%)**. 50 halves both sides; the shape never changes.

## Does resizing make the file smaller?

Usually, yes. File size follows the number of pixels, so halving the width and height leaves a quarter of the pixels and typically a much smaller file. Resizing is often the quickest way to get under an upload limit: a 4,000-pixel phone photo resized to 1920 px wide is far lighter and looks the same on a screen. If you need a specific size in KB, resize first, then use [Compress image](/compress-image/) with a target size.

## Use cases

### Instagram posts and Stories

Instagram crops anything that isn't 1:1, 4:5 or 9:16. Resizing to the preset first means you choose what's kept.

### US visa photo for the DS-160

The online form asks for a square JPEG of at least 600×600 pixels (and at most 240 KB). Use the visa preset with Crop to fill, then [Compress image](/compress-image/) if it's over the size limit. Check the official photo rules for head size; resizing doesn't fix framing.

### YouTube thumbnails

1280×720 is the recommended size; Crop to fill trims a phone photo to 16:9.

### Email signatures and avatars

Small, square images such as 128×128 load quickly in Discord, Slack and email.

### Website images

Resize a 4,000-pixel photo to 1920 px wide (width only) before uploading; it loads faster and looks the same on screen.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this image resizer free?

Yes. No account, no watermark and no limit on the number of images.

### Are my images uploaded?

No. Resizing runs in your browser; the images stay on your device.

### Does resizing reduce quality?

Making an image smaller keeps it sharp. JPG output is saved at quality 90, which looks the same as the original; PNG output is lossless.

### Can I resize many images at once?

Yes. All selected images get the same settings and download as a ZIP.

### Can I make an image bigger?

Yes, up to 400% or 16,000 px, but it will look softer because no new detail can be created. The tool warns you when a file is enlarged.

### Does it keep transparent backgrounds?

Yes, for PNG with Crop to fill or Fit inside. The white-bars option fills the background with white.
