## Quick answer

Drop a PDF, pick a resolution (150 DPI is the default), choose the pages, and press Convert to JPG. Each page becomes one JPG image. One page downloads as a single .jpg; several pages come in a ZIP. At 150 DPI a Letter page is 1275 × 1650 pixels; at 300 DPI it is 2550 × 3300, sharp enough to print.

## How to convert a PDF to JPG

1. **Drop your PDF.** The page count appears next to the file name.
2. **Pick the resolution for where the image is going.** This is the choice people get wrong: 72 DPI looks fine on a phone but blurs when zoomed or printed; 300 DPI prints well but makes big files. 150 DPI suits most screens and email.
3. **Choose the pages.** Leave the full range, or type pages such as `1-3, 7`.
4. **Press Convert to JPG.** Images are named after your file with the page number, for example report-page-03.jpg.

## What DPI should I use for PDF to JPG?

| DPI | Letter page in pixels | A4 page in pixels | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 72 | 612 × 792 | 595 × 842 | Thumbnails, quick previews |
| 150 | 1275 × 1650 | 1240 × 1754 | Screens, email, social posts |
| 200 | 1700 × 2200 | 1654 × 2339 | Reading small print on screen |
| 300 | 2550 × 3300 | 2480 × 3508 | Printing, zooming into detail |

Higher DPI means more pixels and a bigger file. If the image is for a website or chat, 150 DPI is usually enough.

## How do I convert only one page of a PDF to JPG?

Type that page number in the **Pages** box, for example `4`, and press Convert to JPG. You get a single JPG instead of a ZIP. To save a page as a PDF rather than an image, use [Extract PDF pages](/extract-pdf-pages/).

## How do I convert a PDF to JPG on iPhone or Android?

Open this page in Safari or Chrome on your phone, tap **Select a PDF**, and pick the file from Files, Downloads or an email attachment. Convert as usual. A single page saves as a JPG you can add to Photos; several pages arrive as a ZIP. On iPhone, tap the ZIP in the Files app to unzip it, then share the images to Photos. On Android, open the ZIP from Downloads with the Files app. Nothing is uploaded, so it works the same on mobile data.

## JPG or PNG?

JPG files are much smaller and suit photos, scanned pages and anything you will email or post. PNG is lossless, so text edges stay crisp, but files are several times bigger. For slides with small text or diagrams you will edit, use [PDF to PNG](/pdf-to-png/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is PDF to JPG free?

Yes. It is free with no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limit.

### Are my files uploaded?

No. Pages are rendered into images in your browser, on your own device.

### Can I convert all pages at once?

Yes. Leave the full page range and you get every page as a JPG, packed in one ZIP.

### Does a transparent PDF become transparent JPG?

No. JPG has no transparency, so pages are rendered on a white background.

### Will the text in the JPG be searchable?

No. A JPG is a picture of the page. Keep the PDF if you need to search or copy text.

### Why is my ZIP not compressed smaller?

JPG images are already compressed, so the ZIP stores them as they are.
