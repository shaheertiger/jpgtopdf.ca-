## Quick answer

To split PDF pages in half, add the file, choose **Vertical** for left and right halves (book spreads) or **Horizontal** for top and bottom, and press **Split pages in half**. Each page becomes two pages, cut exactly in the middle. You can keep the cover and back cover whole. Nothing is re-rendered, so text stays sharp, and the PDF is never uploaded.

## How to split PDF pages in half

1. **Add the PDF.** Tap **Select a PDF** or drop it on the box.
2. **Choose the cut.** **Vertical** cuts into left and right halves, for open books and booklets. **Horizontal** cuts into top and bottom halves.
3. **Set the reading order.** Tick **Right half first** for right-to-left books (or to put the bottom half first).
4. **Protect the covers.** Most people forget this: a scanned book often starts and ends with a single cover page. Tick **Keep the first page whole** and **Keep the last page whole** so those are not cut.
5. **Press Split pages in half** and download.

## How do I split a scanned book into single pages?

Scan or photograph the book open flat, so each PDF page holds two book pages. Add it here, choose **Vertical**, keep the covers whole if they were scanned alone, and split. The halves come out in reading order: left, right, left, right. If the spreads are tilted or sideways, [rotate](/rotate-pdf/) them first.

## What happens to the other half of each page?

Each half is made by setting the visible area of a copy of the page. The hidden half is still inside the file, just not shown. That keeps text sharp and the file small, but it means the hidden part is not deleted. To remove it for good, turn the pages into images with [Flatten PDF](/flatten-pdf/) afterwards.

| Setting | Each page becomes | Best for |

## Where people use split spreads

### Scanned textbooks and course readers

Single pages read better on a phone or e-reader and are easier to search after running [OCR PDF](/ocr-pdf/).

### Printed booklets and zines

Turn a two-up print file back into single pages for online reading.

### Two forms on one sheet

Offices sometimes print two half-page forms per sheet. A horizontal cut separates them for upload.

### Document submissions

When a portal wants single pages, split the spreads first so each page is readable at full width.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Cut falls off the book's spine | Crop the margins first with [Crop PDF](/crop-pdf/), then split | The cut is always the exact middle of the page |
| Halves in the wrong order | Toggle "Right half first" | The order follows that setting |
| Cover was cut in two | Tick "Keep the first page whole" | The first page is cut by default |
| Spreads are sideways | Rotate the pages first | The cut follows the page as displayed |

## Frequently asked questions

### Does splitting in half blur the pages?

No. Nothing is re-rendered: each half shows part of the original page, so text and images keep their full quality.

### Is the file uploaded?

No. The pages are split by your browser on your own device.

### Can I move the cut line?

No. The cut is always exactly in the middle. If your spreads are off-centre, crop them first so the spine sits in the middle.

### Does it handle rotated pages?

Yes. The cut follows the page as you see it, so left and right are correct even on pages with a rotation setting.

### Is the hidden half really removed?

No. It is hidden, not deleted. Use Flatten PDF afterwards if it must be gone for good.

### Can I split only some pages?

Only the first and last pages can be kept whole. For other single pages, split them all and then remove the extra halves with [Delete PDF pages](/delete-pdf-pages/).
