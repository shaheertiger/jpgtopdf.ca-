## Quick answer

Open the PDF, choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image, then tap the page where it should go. Drag it to move, drag the corner to resize, and press Save PDF. The image is embedded at full resolution, and transparent PNG backgrounds stay transparent. To turn images into new pages instead, use JPG to PDF.

## How to add an image to a PDF

1. **Open the PDF.** Select it or drop it in the box; the first page appears.
2. **Choose the image.** Use "Image to add" to pick a JPG, PNG or WebP file.
3. **Go to the right page.** Use Prev, Next or the page list before you tap. People often place the image on page 1 by mistake.
4. **Tap where it goes.** Drag to move it, drag the bottom corner to resize, ✕ to remove.
5. **Save.** Press "Save PDF". The file downloads as yourfile-with-image.pdf.

## How do I add a logo with no white box around it?

Use a PNG with a transparent background. Transparent areas stay see-through on the page, so only the logo shows. A JPG always has a solid background, usually white, because the format has no transparency.

## Will the image lose quality?

No resizing or recompression happens to a JPG that is already upright: its bytes go straight into the PDF. PNGs are embedded losslessly. A JPG that relies on phone rotation data, a WebP or a GIF is redrawn once at full size, at high quality. Making the box smaller on the page doesn't reduce resolution, so it prints sharply.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Both the PDF and the image stay on your device; everything happens in your browser.

### Can I add several images?

Yes. Tap again to add the same image elsewhere, or choose another image and keep placing.

### Can I put the image behind the text?

No. Images are placed on top of the page content. Use a transparent PNG so the text around it stays visible.

### Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Pick the image from your photos, tap the page, and drag with your finger to move or resize.

### Can I add HEIC photos from an iPhone?

Not directly. Convert them first with [HEIC to JPG](/heic-to-jpg/), then add the JPG.
