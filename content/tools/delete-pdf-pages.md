## Quick answer

To delete pages from a PDF, add the file, then tick the thumbnails of the pages you want gone, or type them in the box, for example "2, 5-7". Press **Delete pages** and download the new file. The pages you keep are copied as they are, so nothing is re-rendered or blurred, and the file is processed in your browser without being uploaded.

## How to delete pages from a PDF

1. **Add the PDF.** Tap **Select a PDF** or drop it on the box. Thumbnails of every page appear.
2. **Pick the pages to remove.** Tick them, or tap a thumbnail to tick it. The box above fills in the ranges for you.
3. **Or type them.** Type "1, 4-6" in **Pages to delete** and the thumbnails tick themselves. Double-check that you typed the pages to delete, not the pages to keep; that is the usual mix-up.
4. **Press Delete pages.** The status line says how many pages were removed and how many remain.
5. **Download** the edited PDF. Your original file is not changed.

## How do I delete a single page from a PDF?

Tick that one page, or type its number, and press **Delete pages**. A 10-page file becomes a 9-page file with every other page untouched.

## How do I remove blank pages from a scan?

Scroll through the thumbnails; blank pages are easy to spot as empty white cards. Tick each one and delete them in one go. If a scanner added a blank back side after every page, typing the ranges is faster, for example "2, 4, 6, 8".

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the PDF uploaded to delete pages?

No. Your browser removes the pages on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server.

### Does deleting pages lower the quality of the rest?

No. The remaining pages are copied without being re-rendered, so they look exactly as before.

### Can I get a deleted page back?

Yes, from your original file, which is never changed. Before saving, just untick the page.

### Can I delete pages on my phone?

Yes, in Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android. Tap a thumbnail to tick it.

### Can I type pages to delete instead of ticking?

Yes. Type numbers and ranges separated by commas, such as "2, 5-7"; the thumbnails update to match.

### Does it work on password-protected PDFs?

No. Remove the password first by saving or printing an unlocked copy in a PDF reader.
