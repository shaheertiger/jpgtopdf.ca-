Organize PDF

Quick answer

To organize a PDF, add the file and you get a thumbnail of every page. Drag a thumbnail to move it, or use the ← and → buttons; tap ↻ to rotate a page 90° clockwise, and ✕ to delete it (tap again to bring it back). Press Save PDF. Pages are copied without re-rendering, so quality stays the same, and the file never leaves your device.

How to organize PDF pages

  1. Add the PDF. Tap Select a PDF or drop it on the box. A thumbnail appears for every page.
  2. Reorder. Drag a page to its new spot. On a phone, or if dragging is fiddly, use ← and → to move a page one place at a time. The numbers update as you go.
  3. Rotate. Tap ↻ on a page to turn it 90° clockwise. Tap again for 180° or 270°.
  4. Delete. Tap ✕ to mark a page for deletion; it fades out. Tap ✕ again to restore it. Nothing is removed until you save.
  5. Check the summary, then save. The line under the pages says how many pages are kept and whether the order or rotation changed. Press Save PDF.

How do I rearrange pages in a PDF?

Drag the page thumbnail and drop it where it belongs: on the left half of another page to go before it, on the right half to go after it. If you prefer buttons, ← moves a page one place earlier and → one place later. The final file follows the order on screen exactly.

To build one document from several files, merge them first, then reorder the merged file here.

How do I delete and rotate pages in the same step?

That is what this page is for. Rotate and delete marks are applied together when you save, in the order on screen. If you only need one of the two jobs, the single-purpose tools are faster on long files: Rotate PDF has "rotate all" buttons and Delete PDF pages lets you type ranges such as "2, 5-7".

Does organizing a PDF change its quality?

No. Each kept page is copied into the new file as it is. Rotation changes the page's rotation setting rather than redrawing the page, so text stays selectable and sharp. The thumbnails are only previews; they are not what gets saved.

Where people use organized PDFs

Scanned paperwork for IRCC or visa portals

Phone scans often come out in the wrong order with a page sideways. Fix both here before uploading, so the officer reads the document in order.

Job application portals

Put the cover letter first, the CV second and certificates last, and remove blank pages a scanner added.

Court e-filing

Exhibits must usually follow your index. Reorder the pages, delete duplicates, then add page numbers with Add page numbers.

School assignment uploads

Photos of handwritten work are often shot out of order. Merge them, reorder them here, and rotate the ones taken sideways.

Contracts sent by email

Remove the instructions page and move the signature page to the end before sending the file to Gmail or Outlook.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded when I organize it?

No. The file is opened and saved by your browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I insert new pages or blank pages?

No. This tool reorders, rotates and deletes the pages that are already in the file. To add pages from another PDF, merge the files first.

Does rotating a page make it blurry?

No. Rotation sets the page's turn angle without redrawing it, so the content stays exactly as sharp as before.

Can I undo a deletion?

Yes, until you save: tap ✕ again on the faded page. After saving, start again from your original file, which is never changed.

Does it work with large PDFs?

Yes, within your device's memory. Thumbnails load as you scroll, so long documents open quickly, though very large files are slower on phones.

Will links and form fields still work?

The visible page content is kept. Interactive form fields and the bookmark list may not carry over reliably, so fill and flatten forms first if they matter.
