Why use a JPG to PDF converter?

JPG is great for photos, but it has real drawbacks when you need to share or submit documents:

  • One file instead of many. A PDF wraps multiple images into a single, tidy document — far easier to email or upload than ten separate photos.
  • It looks the same everywhere. PDFs print and display consistently on any device, so your pages will not shift or resize unexpectedly.
  • It is the standard for official forms. Banks, schools, the CRA and most government portals in Canada expect documents as PDFs, not loose JPGs.
  • It keeps pages in order. A multi-page contract or a set of receipts stays in the sequence you intend.

JPG, JPEG or PNG — what is the difference?

JPG and JPEG are exactly the same format; the name is just shortened on some systems. Our converter accepts both, plus PNG, and you can mix all three in the same PDF. If you are unsure which to use, read PNG to PDF vs JPG to PDF.
