How to Combine Multiple JPG Images into One PDF

Updated June 19, 2026 · About a 4-minute read

Emailing ten separate photos is a hassle for whoever receives them. The fix is to combine multiple JPG images into one PDF — a single, ordered document you can send or upload in one go. Here is how to do it for free, in seconds, without uploading anything.

Combine JPGs into one PDF, step by step

  1. Open the converter. Go to JPGtoPDF.ca in any browser.
  2. Select all your images at once. Tap Select images and choose every JPG you want, or drag them all onto the page together. There is no limit on how many you add.
  3. Put the pages in order. Each image becomes a page. Drag the thumbnails to reorder them, and tap the × on any page you want to remove.
  4. Set page options (optional). Choose Fit to image for an exact fit, or A4 / Letter for uniform pages — handy when mixing portrait and landscape photos.
  5. Download one PDF. Tap Download PDF. Every image is merged into a single document that saves to your device.

When combining images into one PDF is useful

  • Expense reports — merge a month of receipts into one file for your accountant or employer.
  • Multi-page scans — photograph each page of a contract or form and combine them in order.
  • Applications — bundle ID, proof of address and supporting documents into a single upload.
  • Photo sets — turn a series of pictures into one easy-to-share PDF.
  • School and work — submit handwritten notes or whiteboard photos as one tidy document.

Tips for a clean combined PDF

  • Name files in order first. If you select by name, numbering your photos (01, 02, 03) keeps them roughly in sequence before you fine-tune.
  • Use a uniform page size when mixing orientations, so the PDF does not jump between shapes.
  • Crop before converting to remove dark edges from a phone scan and keep the document looking sharp.
  • Mix formats freely. You can combine JPG, JPEG and PNG in the same PDF — each becomes its own page.

Frequently asked questions

How do I combine multiple JPG images into one PDF?

Open JPGtoPDF.ca, select all your JPGs at once, drag the thumbnails to set the order, then tap Download PDF to get a single PDF with each image as a page.

Can I reorder the pages before saving?

Yes. Drag the image thumbnails into any order and remove pages you do not want before downloading.

Is there a limit on how many images I can merge?

No. There is no file limit. Because conversion runs in your browser, very large batches simply take a little longer to process.
