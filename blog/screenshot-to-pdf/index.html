Why Send a Screenshot as a PDF?

A PDF looks like a document: pages in order, the same on every screen, easy to print and file. Banks, landlords and HR departments often accept "a PDF" but quietly distrust a loose image.

What Most People Don't Know

On iPhone, when you screenshot a web page in Safari, you can choose Full Page and save the whole page as a PDF, not just what fits on screen. No stitching, and the text inside stays selectable.

Key point: For web pages on iPhone, use Full Page. For apps, chats and anything else, screenshot then convert.

How to Convert a Screenshot to PDF (Any Device)

Step 2: Crop Before You Convert

Crop out the status bar, notifications, other tabs and anything with account or card numbers. Built-in editors on every device can crop right after the screenshot.

Step 3: Convert

Open jpgtopdf.ca, tap Select images, choose the screenshot(s) in order, and tap Download PDF. Screenshots are usually PNG; they stay lossless and sharp.

Pro tip: Choose Letter if it may be printed. A phone screenshot on a Fit to image page is long and narrow, which prints tiny.

Step 4: Check and Rename

Open the PDF, check every page, and rename it (for example Payment-Confirmation-2026-11-11.pdf).

iPhone: Save a Whole Web Page as PDF (Full Page)

  1. Open the page in Safari and take a screenshot.
  2. Tap the thumbnail in the corner.
  3. Tap Full Page at the top.
  4. Tap Done → Save PDF to Files.

This only works for pages in Safari (and some apps that support it). Labels can change between iOS versions.

Other Built-In Ways

  • Windows: open the screenshot in Photos → Ctrl+P → Microsoft Print to PDF. Windows guide.
  • Mac: open in Preview → File → Export as PDF.
  • Android: Google Photos → ⋮ → Print → Save as PDF. Android guide.
  • Web pages on a computer: in the browser, Ctrl+P / Command+P → Save as PDF gives a real, text-based PDF.

Screenshots as Evidence (Use With Caution)

Screenshots are easy to edit, so some organizations won't accept them as proof. If it matters (a dispute, a claim, a tax question), download the official statement or receipt as a PDF from the source instead.

The Real Risks

  • Rejected as proof because it can't be verified.
  • Shared details you didn't mean to: other messages, balances, names.

Bottom line: Use screenshots for quick proof. For anything official, get the original document from the source.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I turn a screenshot into a PDF?

Select it in a browser converter like jpgtopdf.ca and download the PDF, or use your device's Print to PDF option.

How do I save a screenshot as a PDF on iPhone?

For a Safari web page, tap the screenshot thumbnail, choose Full Page, then Save PDF to Files. For anything else, convert the screenshot with the Files app's Create PDF or a browser converter.

Can I put several screenshots in one PDF?

Yes. Select them in order; each becomes one page of the same PDF.

Is the text in a screenshot PDF searchable?

No. It's an image of text. Use Full Page on iPhone or the browser's Print → Save as PDF for searchable text.

Will the screenshot lose quality?

No. Screenshots are usually PNG, and PNG stays lossless in the PDF.
