There are two ways to save a JPG as a PDF: use the tools built into your device, or use a free online converter that works the same way everywhere. This guide covers both. If you want the quickest, most consistent method — especially for combining several images — the online route wins.

Open JPGtoPDF.ca in your browser, tap Select images, choose your JPG, and tap Download PDF. It works identically on iPhone, Android, Windows and Mac, handles multiple images at once, and never uploads your files. If you only need one device-specific trick, jump to your platform below.

How to save a JPG as a PDF on iPhone

  1. Open the image in the Photos app.
  2. Tap the Share icon, then choose Print.
  3. On the print preview, pinch outward with two fingers on the thumbnail. This opens it as a full PDF.
  4. Tap Share again, then Save to Files and pick a location.

Prefer one tap and the ability to combine photos? Open JPGtoPDF.ca in Safari instead — select your images and download. No pinch trick required.

How to save a JPG as a PDF on Android

  1. Open the photo in Google Photos or your Gallery app.
  2. Tap the menu, choose Print.
  3. In the printer dropdown, select Save as PDF.
  4. Tap the download or save icon and choose where to store it.

Android menus vary by brand (Samsung, Pixel, Xiaomi and so on). For a method that looks the same on every phone, use JPGtoPDF.ca in Chrome.

How to convert a JPG to a PDF on Windows

  1. Double-click the image to open it in the Photos app.
  2. Press Ctrl + P or choose Print.
  3. Under Printer, select Microsoft Print to PDF.
  4. Click Print, name the file, and save.

To merge several JPGs into one PDF on Windows, select them all in File Explorer, right-click and choose Print, then use Microsoft Print to PDF — or just drop them all into our converter and reorder before downloading.

How to save a JPG as a PDF on Mac

  1. Open the image in Preview.
  2. Choose File → Export as PDF.
  3. Name the file and click Save.

To combine images, select them in Finder, right-click and choose Quick Actions → Create PDF, or open them in Preview and drag them into one window.

Which method should you use?

Built-in tools are handy for a single image, but they differ on every device and many cannot combine images easily. A browser-based converter gives you one consistent process, free multi-image merging, page reordering, and complete privacy because nothing is uploaded. For anything beyond a quick one-off, that is the simpler choice.

Frequently asked questions

How do I save a JPG as a PDF on iPhone without an app?

Use the Print → pinch-out trick in Photos, or open JPGtoPDF.ca in Safari and download — both are app-free.

How do I convert a JPG to a PDF on Windows 10 or 11?

Open the image, choose Print, and select Microsoft Print to PDF as the printer. This is built in on both Windows 10 and 11.

Can I save several JPGs as one PDF?

Yes. The easiest way is combining multiple JPGs into one PDF with our converter — select them all and reorder before downloading.
