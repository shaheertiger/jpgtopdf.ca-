Why Combine Images Into One PDF?

Forms and email both prefer one file. A single PDF keeps pages together and in sequence, opens the same way everywhere, and can be printed in one go.

What Most People Don't Know

The PDF's size is roughly the size of all the images added together. Six phone photos at 3 MB each make an 18 MB PDF, too big for many upload forms. Check the total before you combine, not after the upload fails.

Key point: Order and size are the two things that go wrong. Fix the order in the converter, and check the size before uploading.

How to Combine JPGs Into One PDF (Step by Step)

Step 1: Select Every Image at Once

On jpgtopdf.ca, tap Select images and choose all of them. On a computer, Ctrl- or Command-click; on a phone, tap each one. You can add more later by selecting again.

Step 2: Drag Into Order, Don't Trust the File Names

The thumbnails are numbered. Drag them into page order (easiest on a computer). On a phone, select images in the order you want the pages. Remove any extra with the ✕.

Pro tip: For two-sided cards, put front then back. Officers and HR teams read top to bottom; a back side first looks like a missing page.

Step 3: Make the Pages Consistent

Choose Letter (Canada/US) or A4 so every page is the same size, even if the photos aren't. Fit to image keeps each page the size of its photo, which looks uneven when you mix portrait and landscape shots.

Step 4: Download and Check

Tap Download PDF. Open the file, scroll through every page, and check the size before uploading.

Merging PDFs vs Combining Images

If some pages are already PDFs (a bank statement, a signed form), combine your images into one PDF first, then merge the PDFs with a PDF merge tool such as pdfcanada's Merge PDF. Our converter accepts images only.

Upload-Style Merge Sites (Use With Caution)

Merging is often done to send identity or financial documents, exactly what you don't want sitting on a stranger's server.

The Real Risks

  • Server copies of IDs, leases and pay stubs.
  • Page caps on free tiers (often 20 pages or 2 files).
  • Watermarks added to every page.

Checklist: browser-only processing, no sign-up, no watermark, clear deletion policy.

Bottom line: Combine images on your own device. It's faster and nothing leaves it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I combine JPEGs into one PDF for free?

Select all the JPEGs in a browser converter, arrange them and download one PDF. Windows, Mac and iPhone also have built-in options.

How many images can I put in one PDF?

Our converter has no set limit; very large batches depend on your device's memory. Upload portals usually limit the file size, not the page count.

Can I mix JPG and PNG in the same PDF?

Yes. Each image becomes its own page regardless of format.

How do I change the page order?

Drag the numbered thumbnails before downloading. In built-in tools, rename files so they sort in the right order.

Why is my combined PDF so big?

Its size is roughly the total of all the images. Resize or compress them to bring it down.

Can I add an image to an existing PDF?

Convert the image to PDF, then merge the two PDFs with a merge tool, or rebuild the PDF from all the images.
