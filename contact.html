Contact us

We’d love to hear from you. Whether you have a question, found a bug, or have an idea to make the JPG to PDF converter better, get in touch.

Email

Write to us at contact@jpgtopdf.ca and we’ll reply as soon as we can, usually within a few business days.

What to include

  • A clear description of your question or the problem you ran into.
  • The device and browser you were using (for example, iPhone Safari or Windows Chrome).
  • Any steps that help us reproduce a bug.

Please do not send the images or documents you were converting — because conversion happens entirely in your browser, we never receive your files and cannot access them.
