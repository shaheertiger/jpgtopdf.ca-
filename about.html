About JPGtoPDF.ca

JPGtoPDF.ca is a free online tool that converts JPG, JPEG and PNG images into a single PDF. It was built for one reason: turning images into a PDF should be quick, private, and free — without uploading personal documents to a stranger’s server.

How it works

Every conversion happens locally in your web browser using JavaScript and the open-source jsPDF library. Your images are read on your own device, assembled into a PDF, and downloaded straight to you. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party, which is why the tool keeps working even when you go offline after the page has loaded.

Who we are

JPGtoPDF.ca is a project of pdfcanada.ca, a small Canadian team that builds simple, dependable document utilities. We focus on tools that respect your privacy and load fast on any device.

What it costs

The tool is completely free, with no account, no watermark and no file limit. To keep it free, the site may display advertising. You can read how that works in our Privacy Policy.
