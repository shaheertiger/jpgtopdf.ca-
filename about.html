About JPGtoPDF.ca

JPGtoPDF.ca started as a free JPG to PDF converter and is now a set of free PDF and image tools: JPG to PDF, Compress PDF, Merge PDF, OCR PDF, Sign PDF and more. They were built for one reason: everyday document jobs should be quick, private and free, without uploading personal documents to a stranger’s server.

How it works

Every tool runs locally in your web browser using JavaScript and open-source libraries served from our own domain: pdf-lib, Mozilla’s PDF.js, Tesseract for OCR and heic2any for iPhone photos. Your files are read on your own device, processed there, and downloaded straight back to you. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party, which is why the tools keep working when you go offline after a page has loaded. How it works has the details and each tool’s limits.

Who we are

JPGtoPDF.ca is a project of pdfcanada.ca, a small Canadian team that builds simple, dependable document utilities. We focus on tools that respect your privacy and load fast on any device.

What it costs

The tools are completely free, with no account and no watermark. To keep it free, the site may display advertising. You can read how that works in our Privacy Policy.
