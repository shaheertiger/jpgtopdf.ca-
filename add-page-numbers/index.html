Add page numbers

Quick answer

Open the PDF, choose a position (bottom centre is the default), a format such as "Page 1 of N", a size and the distance from the edge, then press Add page numbers. To skip a cover, set "Start on page" to 2. Numbers are added as real text, so they print sharply and the rest of the page is unchanged.

How to add page numbers to a PDF

  1. Open the PDF. Select it or drop it in the box.
  2. Pick the position and format. Bottom centre with "1" suits most documents; "Page 1 of N" suits reports and contracts.
  3. Set where numbering starts. This is the step people miss: with a cover page, set "Start on page" to 2 and "First number" to 1, so page 2 shows 1. The line under the options tells you what page one will show.
  4. Choose size and distance from the edge. 11 pt at 12 mm works for most printers.
  5. Download. Press "Add page numbers". The file saves as yourfile-numbered.pdf.

How do I skip the cover page?

Set "Start on page" to 2. Page 1 gets no number. Then decide what page 2 shows: "First number" 1 makes it page 1; 2 keeps the physical count. "Page 1 of N" counts only the numbered pages.

Common uses

Court and legal bundles

Many courts and tribunals ask for numbered pages. Merge the documents first, then number the whole bundle with "Page 1 of N".

University submissions

Theses and assignments often skip the title page: start on page 2.

Printed booklets

Use "Bottom outside (book)" so numbers sit on the outer edge when printed double-sided.

Frequently asked questions

Will the numbers change my document?

Only the numbers are added. Text, images and links stay exactly as they were.

Do numbers appear on rotated pages correctly?

Yes. They are drawn upright as the page is displayed, in the right corner, even on landscape or rotated pages.

Can I use a different font or colour?

No. Numbers use black Helvetica, which every PDF reader displays the same way.

Can I add Roman numerals?

No. Only digits are supported.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser and the file stays on your device.

Is it free?

Yes, with no account and no watermark.
