HEIC to JPG

Quick answer

Drop one or more HEIC photos into the box, choose JPG (quality 90 by default) or PNG, and press Convert to JPG. One photo downloads as a JPG; several download together as a ZIP. The conversion happens in your browser with Safari's built-in decoder or a self-hosted copy of libheif, so your photos are never uploaded.

How to convert HEIC to JPG

  1. Select your HEIC files. Drop them in, or tap the box and choose them from Files or a folder.
  2. Choose the format. JPG works everywhere and is far smaller. PNG is lossless but often five or more times bigger.
  3. Pick the JPG quality. High (90) is the default and looks identical to the original for normal viewing. Use Maximum (95) for editing, Medium (80) to save space.
  4. Convert and download. Press Convert to JPG. With more than one photo you get a ZIP; unzip it to get the JPG files.

Why does my iPhone take HEIC photos?

Since iOS 11, the iPhone camera saves photos as HEIC by default because it takes roughly half the space of a JPEG at similar quality. Many Windows PCs, Android phones, websites and upload forms still can't open it. You can make the iPhone shoot JPG instead under Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible, but photos already taken stay HEIC.

Frequently asked questions

Is HEIC to JPG free?

Yes. No account, no watermark and no limit on the number of photos.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser; the photos never leave your device.

Will the JPG lose quality?

Very little. At quality 90 the JPG looks the same as the HEIC to the eye; choose 95 if you plan to edit it.

Is the photo's location (GPS) kept?

No. The JPG is drawn fresh from the decoded image, so camera and location details are not copied into it.

Can I convert many HEIC files at once?

Yes. Add as many as your device can handle; they download together as one ZIP.

Does it work on Windows without the HEIF extension?

Yes. In Chrome, Edge or Firefox the page loads its own decoder, so no Microsoft Store extension is needed.
