Sign PDF

Quick answer

Open the PDF, then draw your signature with a finger, pen or mouse, type your name, or upload a photo of your signature. Tap the page where it goes, drag to move it, drag the corner to resize, and save. It takes about a minute. The result is a visible electronic signature, not a certificate-based digital signature.

How to sign a PDF

  1. Open the PDF. Tap "Select a PDF" or drop the file in the box. The first page appears below.
  2. Create your signature. Choose Draw, Type or Upload. Draw it in the box, or type your name and pick a style, or choose a photo of your signature on white paper.
  3. Press "Use this signature". This is the step people skip: nothing can be placed until you press it.
  4. Tap the page where it goes. Use Prev, Next or the page list to reach the right page. Drag to move it, drag the bottom corner to resize, and use ✕ to remove it.
  5. Add the date if the form asks for it. Press "Add today's date", then tap where the date goes.
  6. Save. Press "Save signed PDF". The file downloads as yourfile-signed.pdf.

Is a drawn signature legally valid?

In many countries, including Canada, the US and the EU, an electronic signature is generally accepted for everyday documents such as leases, consent forms, offer letters and invoices, as long as the other side agrees to it. Some documents, such as wills, certain property deeds and some court or government forms, still need a wet-ink signature or a specific process. If you are unsure, ask the person or office that sent you the document.

What this tool adds is a picture of your signature on the page. It does not add a digital certificate, so a PDF reader will not show a "signed and verified" banner. If a form asks for a certificate-based digital signature (sometimes called a qualified or advanced signature), use the signer's own certificate tool, such as the one your bank, employer or government provides.

How do I sign a PDF on my phone?

Open this page in Safari or Chrome on your phone and pick the PDF from Files, Drive or your email download. Drawing with a finger works well: turn the phone sideways for a wider pad. The finished PDF saves to your downloads, ready to send back by email or upload to a portal. No app is needed.

How do I make my signature look natural?

Draw slowly and a little larger than your real signature; it is scaled down when you place it. A stylus gives a thinner, more natural line. If you upload a photo instead, sign in dark pen on plain white paper, in good light, and crop the photo close to the signature. The white background is removed, so the page shows through around the ink.

Common situations

Rental lease or offer letter

Sign on the signature line, add the date beside it, and send the PDF back. If there are initials on every page, place your signature (smaller) on each page.

School and consent forms

Permission slips and medical consent forms usually only need a visible signature and the date.

Invoices and delivery notes

Add your signature as proof of approval or receipt, then merge it with other paperwork if you send everything together.

Forms you have filled in

Fill in the form first, then sign. If you want the answers locked so they can't be edited, run Flatten PDF on the signed copy.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a digital signature?

No. It is a visible electronic signature: an image of your signature placed on the page. It does not include a digital certificate, so PDF readers will not show it as cryptographically verified.

Is my document uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is opened and saved inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, which matters for contracts, IDs and medical forms.

Can I sign more than one page?

Yes. Move to another page with Prev, Next or the page list and tap again. Each tap adds the current signature, so you can sign or initial every page.

Can other people sign the same PDF?

Yes. Send them the signed file; they can open it here and add their own signature in the same way.

Can I remove a signature after saving?

Not with this tool. Keep your original unsigned PDF, and sign it again if you need to change something.

Does it work on rotated or scanned pages?

Yes. The signature is placed upright as the page appears on screen, including pages that are rotated or scanned.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no account, no limit on how many documents you sign, and no watermark.
