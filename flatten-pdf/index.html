Flatten PDF — Lock Forms, Comments and Layers

Open a PDF, choose forms only or everything, download the flat copy.

Flatten PDF

Quick answer

Open the PDF and choose "Form fields only" to bake your answers into the page while text stays selectable, or "Everything" to turn every page into an image at 150, 200 or 300 DPI, which also flattens comments, stamps, signatures and layers. Everything mode makes text unselectable, so keep your original. The tool shows how many form fields it found.

How to flatten a PDF

  1. Open the PDF. The tool counts the fillable fields it finds and picks a mode for you.
  2. Choose what to flatten. "Form fields only" for a filled-in form; "Everything" for comments, highlights, stamps or layers.
  3. Pick a resolution for Everything. 200 DPI is the default; 300 DPI for printing; 150 DPI for a smaller file.
  4. Download. Press "Flatten PDF". Open the result and check the answers and signatures look right before you send it.

What does flattening a PDF do?

A filled-in PDF form keeps answers in editable fields that anyone can change. Comments, stamps and drawn signatures sit on separate layers that can be moved or deleted. Flattening merges them into the page itself so the document looks the same everywhere and can't be casually edited.

Common uses

Tax and government forms

Fill in a form such as a W-9 or a benefits application, flatten the fields, and send a copy nobody can change by accident.

After signing

Sign with Sign PDF, then flatten everything so the signature can't be lifted off as a separate object.

After cropping or redacting

Crop PDF only hides what is outside the box. Flattening everything keeps only what is visible.

Annotated drafts

Bake reviewer comments and highlights into a version for the record.

Frequently asked questions

Can I undo flattening?

No. Keep the original file if you may need to edit it again.

Does forms-only flattening change the look?

Usually not; the answers stay where they were. A few fields may render with a slightly different font.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.

Will links still work?

In forms-only mode, yes. In Everything mode the page is an image, so links stop working.

Does flattening remove hidden content?

Everything mode does: only what is visible on the page is kept. Forms-only mode leaves the rest of the file as it was.

Is it free?

Yes, with no account and no watermark.
