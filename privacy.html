Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026.

Your files stay on your device

Every tool on JPGtoPDF.ca (JPG to PDF, Compress PDF, Merge, OCR and the others) processes your files entirely inside your web browser using JavaScript. Your images and PDFs are never uploaded to any server, never stored, and never seen by us or anyone else. When you close the tab, nothing remains.

The first time you use some tools, your browser downloads the program code they need (for example the PDF library, or the text-recognition engine and language data for OCR) from our own domain. This is the same as loading any other part of the page: your files are not part of these requests. Your browser may cache this code so the tools work offline later. How it works explains the details.

What we collect

We do not require an account and do not collect personal information through the tools. We use Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies, to count page views and, on tool pages, that a tool was used (the tool’s name only). It never includes your files, their names or their contents.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertising to keep the tool free. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.

Children’s privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@jpgtopdf.ca, or through our Contact page.
