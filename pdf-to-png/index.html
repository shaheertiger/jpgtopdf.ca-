PDF to PNG

Quick answer

Drop a PDF, choose a resolution and the pages, and press Convert to PNG. Each page becomes a lossless PNG, so small text and thin lines stay crisp with no JPG blur. The trade-off is size: PNG files are several times bigger than JPG. One page downloads as a .png; several come in a ZIP. Pages are rendered on a white background.

How to convert a PDF to PNG

  1. Drop your PDF. Its page count appears.
  2. Choose the resolution. 150 DPI suits screens. Pick 300 DPI for print or for slides you will zoom into; the files get much bigger.
  3. Set the pages. Keep the full range or type pages such as 1, 4-6.
  4. Press Convert to PNG. Files are named like report-page-04.png.

When is PNG better than JPG?

PNG keeps every pixel exactly, so it is the better choice for text-heavy pages, charts, diagrams, line drawings and screenshots you plan to edit or annotate. JPG is better for photos and scanned pages, and whenever file size matters. If size is the problem, use PDF to JPG instead.

Is the PNG background transparent?

No. Pages are drawn on white, the way they look in a PDF reader. If you need a transparent background, you will have to remove the white in an image editor afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Is PDF to PNG free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no watermark.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. The pages are rendered in your browser and saved straight to your device.

Is PNG lossless?

Yes. PNG stores the rendered page without compression artifacts, which is why it is bigger than JPG.

Can I convert one page only?

Yes. Type that page number in the Pages box and you get a single PNG.

How big is a page at 300 DPI?

A Letter page is 2550 × 3300 pixels and an A4 page 2480 × 3508 pixels.

Can I get the images that are inside the PDF?

No. This tool renders whole pages. It does not pull out the original embedded photos.
